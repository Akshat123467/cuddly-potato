Executive Valuation Overview & Biography

Dilip Shanghvi holds Global Rank #16 with an independent estimated net worth of $26.9B USD (₹2.54 Lakh Cr / ₹2,53,640 Cr) as of November 14, 2026. Wealth is anchored primarily by equity ownership in Sun Pharmaceutical Industries.

Dilip Shanghvi founded Sun Pharmaceutical Industries in 1983 in Vapi, Gujarat, with just ₹10,000 borrowed from his father. Through disciplined acquisitions including Caraco, Taro, and Ranbaxy Laboratories, he turned Sun Pharma into India's largest pharmaceutical company and a global specialty generics leader.
