Independent Net Worth Calculation Methodology

BillionaireWatch.in publishes independent mathematical estimates computed from primary regulatory documents and open market data (without affiliation to Forbes Media or Bloomberg L.P.):

  • Public Company Stakes: Share counts reported on SEC EDGAR (Form 4, Schedule 13D/13G) and Indian BSE/NSE promoter filings multiplied by closing exchange quotes.
  • Vested Performance Options: In-the-money spread computed as (Market Price − Option Strike Price) × vested option count.
  • Private Company Holdings: Benchmarked against disclosed tender offers, primary institutional financing rounds, and comparable listed multiples.
  • Currency Conversion: Reference USD/INR exchange rates (94.29 INR per USD as of this snapshot) applied consistently across global and Indian profiles.
