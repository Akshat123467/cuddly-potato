Independent Methodology & Non-Affiliation Notice

All net worth figures published on BillionaireWatch.in are independent mathematical estimates prepared strictly for educational and informational research—never investment, legal, or tax advice. Public equity positions are computed from SEC EDGAR beneficial ownership reports (Form 4 / Schedule 13D) and Indian BSE/NSE promoter shareholding disclosures multiplied by public closing stock prices. Vested performance options and private enterprises are benchmarked against secondary tender-offer prices and peer multiples.

Real-world private debt, trusts, and tax obligations are non-public and can cause actual wealth to differ materially from any public estimate.
