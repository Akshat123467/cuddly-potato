Executive Valuation Overview & Biography

Warren Buffett holds Global Rank #7 with an independent estimated net worth of $186.5B USD (₹17.59 Lakh Cr / ₹17,58,509 Cr) as of November 14, 2026. Wealth is anchored primarily by equity ownership in Berkshire Hathaway.

Warren Buffett transformed Berkshire Hathaway into a trillion-dollar conglomerate spanning GEICO, BNSF Railway, Berkshire Energy, and major equity stakes in Apple, American Express, and Coca-Cola. Even after donating more than $55 billion of Berkshire shares to philanthropy, his Class A stake places him firmly among the world's top ten richest individuals.
